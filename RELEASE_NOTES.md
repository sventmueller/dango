## AI tab
- `/resume` opens an overlay that lists the agent's past sessions in this folder; the one you choose continues in a tab of its own.
- Agents and Copilot now learn Dango's real version instead of 0.0.0.

## Terminal
- The terminal runs on a new screen engine: it redraws only the lines that changed, and its scrollback takes less memory while the program in it is idle.

## Tabs
- A tab opened from the + menu or with ⌘T appears at the end of the tab bar instead of next to the one in front.

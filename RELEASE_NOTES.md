## AI tab
- Hooks that ran fine or are still running no longer show in the timeline, not even as a collapsed row per reply; a hook that fails still shows.

## Terminal
- When a busy Mac reads a program's output late, the terminal still notices that the program ended and keeps its last lines.

## Markdown
- A formula with a backtick in it, pasted from Word or a web page, stays a formula instead of turning into code.
- Pictures in bold or italic text keep both in the Word export.
- A file with more than 500,000 empty table cells shows its tables as text instead of taking seconds and gigabytes of memory.

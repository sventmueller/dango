## Text sizes
- A new setting, "Sidebar and tab font size", sets the sidebar, its symbols and the tab bars from 10 to 18 points.
  Every panel of the sidebar, the activity bar, the window's tab titles and the panes' own tab bars grow and shrink
  in proportion: text, symbols, rows and the room between them. Open windows follow at once.
- The editor and terminal font size now reaches open editors at once, with their Markdown styles and line numbers,
  and the Markdown preview grows and shrinks in the same proportion.
- The head of an AI tab follows the AI tab's text size, like its timeline, message field and status line.

## AI
- Codex sessions list their subagents and background terminals behind the two buttons in the tab's head, as Claude
  sessions do; a subagent's permission requests and its stop button work on their own.
- The button that shows only the conversation now sits between the background processes' button and the bookmark.
